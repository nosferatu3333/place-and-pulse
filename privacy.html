Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you submit the "Build your trip" form, we collect what you enter: your name, travel dates, guest count, trip style, accommodation budget, email address, and any notes you add. We don't use cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts on this site — nothing is collected just by browsing.

How it's used

Submitting the form does two things at once: it sends your details to our concierge team so we can start planning, and it opens a prefilled WhatsApp message so you can reach us directly if you'd like. We use this information only to plan and coordinate your trip — to check availability, put together options, and follow up with you.

Who sees it

Your information is seen by our concierge team. We share only what's necessary with specific villas, transport, dining, or experience providers once you've asked us to move forward with a booking — never before, and never for marketing purposes. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How long we keep it

We keep trip inquiries only as long as needed to respond to you and, if you book, to deliver the trip. If you'd like us to delete your information, email us and we'll take care of it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time — just reach out using the contact details below.
